Frequently Asked Questions

Do you accept walk-ins or are appointments required?

Walk-ins are welcomed Tuesday through Saturday from 9:00 AM to 4:00 PM! To guarantee immediate chair availability with your preferred barber, we recommend reserving on Booksy.

Where are you located and is there parking available?

We are located at 4960 W Charleston Blvd, Las Vegas, NV 89146 in Charleston Decatur Center. We have ample, free parking directly in front of the shop.

Do you offer braiding and locs services?

Yes! Hairanesa specializes in professional men's and women's cornrows, protective braid styling, and loc maintenance alongside master precision tapers.

What payment methods are accepted?

We accept all major credit/debit cards (Visa, Mastercard, AMEX, Discover), Apple Pay, Google Pay, Booksy direct pay, and cash.

What is included with a signature haircut?

Every signature haircut includes a consultation, precision clipper/scissor cut, hot lather razor neck cleanup, hair styling with premium products, and aftershave tonic.
